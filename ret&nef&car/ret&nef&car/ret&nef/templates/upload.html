About Us

Our web application is dedicated to helping individuals detect various complications associated with diabetes. The core features of our application include:

  • Users need to log in or register to access the system.
  • Users can upload their medical scans for analysis.
  • Our system provides predictions of the uploaded scans.
  • We also offer preventive measures based on the analysis results.
